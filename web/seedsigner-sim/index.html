The upstream SeedSigner firmware (Python, pinned in docs/architecture/BASELINES.md) running verbatim in your browser via Pyodide — the emulator is the firmware. QR scanning uses your webcam (or a dropped image) decoded by jsQR; exports can be handed off straight to the SeedHammer v1 emulator. Nothing leaves your browser; even so, do not enter a real seed in a simulator.
